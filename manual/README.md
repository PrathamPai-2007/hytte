# Hytte manual

Hytte is a small pill at the top of your Windows 11 screen. It stays out of the way until something worth knowing happens, then opens, shows it, and tucks itself away.

New here? Read the first two pages, then pick what you need.

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|

| [2. The pill](02-the-pill.md) | Opening it, moving between panes, and what each look means. |
| [3. Your terminal](03-terminal.md) | Seeing long commands on the pill, live cards from your scripts, and your own panes. |
| [4. AI agents](04-ai-agents.md) | Following Claude Code, Codex and others, and answering their prompts. |
| [5. Shelf and file actions](05-shelf.md) | Parking files, and one-click image, text and PDF tools. |
| [6. Everyday tools](06-everyday-tools.md) | Music and lyrics, quick controls, timer and focus mode, notes, to-dos, weather, break reminders, dev servers and alerts. |
| [7. Settings](07-settings.md) | Changing how Hytte looks and behaves. |
| [8. Updates and troubleshooting](08-updates-and-troubleshooting.md) | Staying current, fixing problems, and uninstalling. |
| [9. Ask (local AI)](09-ask.md) | Ask a small model on your own PC, and Explain / Summarise / Shorten a clip, file or failed command. |
